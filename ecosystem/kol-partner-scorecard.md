# KOL & Partner Scorecard

## Red flags
- Artificial engagement
- Repeated undisclosed promotions
- Brand-safety issues
- Low audience relevance
- Poor communication history
- Inconsistent delivery

## Decision
**Priority:** High / Medium / Low  
**Recommended activation:** AMA / campaign / referral / content / event / education
