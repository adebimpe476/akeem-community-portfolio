# Ecosystem Collaboration Checklist

## Before announcing
- Confirm scope with partner
- Verify names, dates and links
- Align messaging
- Identify member value
- Define CTA
- Confirm support/escalation owner

## Activation
- Announcement
- Moderator briefing
- KOL/creator support
- AMA/live session where useful
- Community FAQ
- Reminder cadence
- Social amplification

## After activation
- Participation metrics
- Community sentiment
- Partner feedback
- Support issues
- Follow-up opportunity
- Reuseable learnings

## Principle
A partnership announcement is not an activation. Activation requires a clear behavior members can take.
