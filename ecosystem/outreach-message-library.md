# Partnership & KOL Outreach Message Library

These are structures, not copy-paste spam.

## Partnership opener
Hi [Name] — I manage community/growth work around [context]. I noticed [specific relevance]. There may be a useful collaboration around [specific idea] that gives your audience [value] and ours [value]. If relevant, I can send a one-page activation concept.

## KOL opener
Hi [Name] — your work on [specific topic] overlaps strongly with our community around [topic]. We’re planning [AMA/campaign/education initiative] and I think your perspective could be valuable. Interested in exploring a simple collaboration?

## Follow-up
Following up on this because the fit is specifically around [reason], not a generic sponsorship ask. Happy to send the activation outline if useful.

## Rule
Personalize the first two lines. If they could be sent unchanged to 100 people, the message is too generic.
