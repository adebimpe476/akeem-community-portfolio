# Affiliate Partnership Playbook

Inspired by practical affiliate-partnership work in trading communities.

## Workflow
1. Identify a product with genuine community relevance
2. Validate reputation and user fit
3. Define value exchange
4. Initiate direct outreach
5. Agree tracking and attribution
6. Prepare community-facing education
7. Launch without overpromising
8. Monitor conversions and complaints
9. Optimize or stop based on evidence

## Activation assets
- Partner introduction
- FAQ
- Community announcement
- Educational content
- Tracking link
- Disclosure statement
- Support escalation route

## Metrics
- Click-through
- Qualified registrations
- Activation
- Conversion
- Retention
- Member feedback
- Support incidents
