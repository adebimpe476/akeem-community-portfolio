# Partnership Pipeline Template

## Pipeline stages
**Target → Qualified → Outreach Sent → Conversation → Proposal → Agreed → Activation → Review**

| Partner | Type | Audience fit | Value exchange | Stage | Owner | Next action | Date |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example | Ecosystem | High | Co-marketing | Qualified | Community | Send brief | — |

## Qualification criteria
Score 1–5:
- Audience relevance
- Reputation/trust
- Engagement quality
- Strategic fit
- Distribution potential
- Ease of activation
- Long-term value

## Weekly review
- New qualified partners
- Stalled conversations
- Activations launching
- Performance from live partnerships
- Follow-ups due
