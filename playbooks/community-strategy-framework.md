# Community Strategy Framework

> Portfolio framework by Akeem Adebimpe — designed to show how I structure community growth from acquisition to advocacy.

## 1. Community objective
Every community needs a clear business and member objective. I define:
- Who the community is for
- What members should gain
- What the organization should gain
- Which behaviors signal a healthy community

## 2. Lifecycle model
**Acquire → Onboard → Activate → Engage → Retain → Amplify → Measure → Learn**

### Acquire
Channels: partnerships, KOLs, social campaigns, referrals, content and community collaborations.

### Onboard
Deliver a clear welcome path, community expectations, product education and a first useful action.

### Activate
Move members quickly into a meaningful behavior: contribute, attend, post, refer, test, report, learn or participate.

### Engage
Use recurring programming: AMAs, challenges, live sessions, campaigns, recognition and peer interaction.

### Retain
Track inactive cohorts, collect feedback, improve member experience and create reasons to return.

### Amplify
Turn the strongest members into advocates, contributors, ambassadors and community voices.

## 3. Operating rhythm
| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Moderation, support, conversations, issue triage |
| Weekly | Campaigns, recognition, content, reporting |
| Biweekly | Live session / AMA / community program |
| Monthly | Health review, retention review, partnership/KOL review |
| Quarterly | Community strategy reset and roadmap |

## 4. Health metrics
- Active member rate
- New-member activation rate
- Retention / reactivation
- Campaign participation
- Event attendance
- Response time
- Member sentiment
- Contributor output
- Referral / advocacy activity

## 5. Feedback loop
Community insight should flow into **product, operations, content and growth**. Recurring issues are tagged, summarized and routed to the right owner with evidence and suggested action.
