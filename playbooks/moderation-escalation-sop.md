# Moderation & Escalation SOP

> Portfolio SOP for maintaining healthy, useful and safe online communities.

## Moderation goals
- Protect members
- Keep conversation useful
- Reduce spam and scams
- Resolve conflict early
- Preserve brand trust
- Escalate real issues quickly

## Severity levels
| Level | Example | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Off-topic / minor disruption | Redirect / remind |
| 2 | Repeated guideline breach | Warning / temporary restriction |
| 3 | Harassment / scam / impersonation | Remove content, restrict, document |
| 4 | Security / legal / serious threat | Immediate escalation to leadership |

## Moderator workflow
**Observe → Verify → Act → Document → Escalate if needed → Follow up**

## Good moderation principles
- Apply rules consistently
- Separate disagreement from abuse
- Avoid public arguments with members
- Document serious incidents
- Protect private information
- Use warnings when appropriate
- Keep escalation channels clear

## Community-health signals
- Repeat complaints
- Spam frequency
- Response time
- Sentiment shifts
- Unanswered questions
- Conflict hotspots
- Moderator workload

The objective is not to silence disagreement. It is to maintain an environment where useful participation can continue.
