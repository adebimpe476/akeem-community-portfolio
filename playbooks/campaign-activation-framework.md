# Community Campaign Activation Framework

> A practical structure for moving a campaign from idea to measurable community action.

## Campaign brief
Every campaign starts with:
- Objective
- Target community segment
- Desired behavior
- Core message
- Incentive / reason to participate
- Channel mix
- Timeline
- Success metric

## Activation sequence
### 1. Prime
Educate members before the main ask.

### 2. Launch
Use one clear CTA with simple participation rules.

### 3. Mobilize
Coordinate moderators, contributors, ambassadors and KOLs.

### 4. Sustain
Use reminders, progress updates, recognition and social proof.

### 5. Close
Publish results, reward contributors and capture learnings.

## Useful campaign types
- Referral campaigns
- Social amplification / raids
- Product education
- Challenges
- Giveaways
- Community recognition
- UGC prompts
- Event registration
- Reactivation campaigns

## Reporting template
| Metric | What it tells us |
|---|---|
| Reach | How many people saw the activation |
| Participants | How many acted |
| Conversion | How many completed the target action |
| Engagement | Depth of participation |
| Retention | Whether participants remained active |
| Sentiment | How members felt about the campaign |

## Principle
The best community campaign is not the loudest one. It is the one that makes the desired action obvious, meaningful and easy.
