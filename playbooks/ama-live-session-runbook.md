# AMA & Live Session Runbook

> Portfolio runbook for AMAs, X Spaces, webinars, community calls and live Q&A sessions.

## Before the session
- Confirm objective and audience
- Research speaker / guest
- Prepare opening, key questions and follow-ups
- Collect community questions
- Confirm timing and roles
- Publish reminders
- Test links / audio / stream
- Prepare moderation rules and escalation plan

## Suggested agenda
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Welcome, context, speaker intro |
| 5–20 min | Core discussion |
| 20–35 min | Deep-dive questions |
| 35–50 min | Community Q&A |
| 50–55 min | Key takeaways |
| 55–60 min | CTA and close |

## Host responsibilities
- Keep energy and structure
- Translate complex points for the audience
- Watch chat sentiment
- Prevent one person from dominating
- Ask useful follow-ups
- Protect speaker and community safety
- Keep the session on time

## After the session
- Share recap
- Clip key moments
- Answer unanswered questions
- Track attendance / engagement
- Collect feedback
- Convert insights into follow-up content or product feedback
