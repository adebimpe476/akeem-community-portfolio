# KOL & Partnership Framework

> Portfolio framework showing how I approach creator, KOL, affiliate and ecosystem relationships.

## Partner / KOL selection
I assess:
1. Audience relevance
2. Community credibility
3. Engagement quality
4. Brand fit
5. Geographic / ecosystem reach
6. Collaboration history
7. Risk / reputation

## Outreach structure
**Context → Relevance → Value → Specific ask → Easy next step**

A strong outreach message should answer:
- Why this person / organization?
- Why now?
- What is in it for both sides?
- What exactly are we proposing?

## Activation options
- AMA / live session
- Co-branded campaign
- Community challenge
- Referral / affiliate activation
- Creator education
- KOL amplification
- Community swap / cross-promotion
- Product walkthrough
- Launch support

## Partnership operating flow
**Identify → Qualify → Outreach → Align → Activate → Measure → Deepen**

## Measurement
- Reach
- Qualified community growth
- Engagement
- Registrations / sign-ups
- Participation
- Referral activity
- Content performance
- Repeat collaboration potential

## Relevant experience
My portfolio includes direct affiliate-partnership outreach through TradesbyTCG, KOL/influencer relationship work at Dogelon Mars, and community-facing activation around ecosystem collaboration.
