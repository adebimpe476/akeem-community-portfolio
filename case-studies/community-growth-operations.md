# Community Growth & Operations — Case Study

## Objective
Demonstrate how I connect acquisition, onboarding, activation, engagement and retention instead of treating each as a separate activity.

## Selected impact
- Managed an international Web3 community of 11,000+ members.
- Mobilized 2,000+ participants in coordinated campaigns.
- Grew a Telegram community by 3,000+ members in two months.
- Built an organic Instagram audience of 10,000+.
- Worked in a QuikEco operating environment showing 191 reporters, 112 active reporters, 1,368 verified reports and 92% approval.

## Operating model
**Acquire → Onboard → Activate → Engage → Retain → Amplify → Measure → Learn**

## Example responsibilities
- Build onboarding flows and first-action pathways.
- Run campaigns, live sessions and community programs.
- Re-engage inactive members.
- Coordinate moderators and contributors.
- Track community health and recurring member issues.
- Turn feedback into actions for growth, operations and product teams.

## Reporting structure
| Area | Question |
|---|---|
| Growth | Are the right members joining? |
| Activation | Are new members taking a meaningful first action? |
| Engagement | Are members participating consistently? |
| Retention | Are they returning? |
| Advocacy | Are members referring and amplifying? |
| Operations | What friction is blocking the community? |
