# Partnerships & KOL Operations — Case Study

## Objective
Show how community, partnerships and creator relationships can work together as one growth system.

## Relevant experience

### TradesbyTCG — VoltFX partnership
- Identified an affiliate partnership opportunity aligned with the trading community.
- Initiated direct outreach to VoltFX.
- Developed the partnership relationship.
- Supported activation through community communication and social distribution.

### Dogelon Mars — KOL and ecosystem activation
- Built relationships with KOLs, influencers and external community voices.
- Supported KOL acquisition and outreach initiatives.
- Coordinated community-driven amplification campaigns.
- Supported community communication around ecosystem collaboration involving Wintermute and BloFin.

## Operating framework
**Identify → Qualify → Outreach → Align → Activate → Measure → Deepen**

### What I measure
- Relevant reach
- Qualified community growth
- Engagement
- Registrations / sign-ups
- Campaign participation
- Referral activity
- Repeat collaboration potential

## Why this matters
A strong community manager should understand how to turn external relationships into useful member experiences, not just announcements. Partnerships work best when they create a clear benefit for both the partner and the community.
