# Moderator Handbook

## Moderator mission
Protect useful participation, trust and member safety.

## Daily responsibilities
- Welcome and orient members
- Answer common questions
- Remove spam/scams
- Apply guidelines consistently
- De-escalate conflict
- Escalate serious incidents
- Surface recurring issues
- Support campaigns and live programs

## Tone
Calm, clear, respectful and firm.

## Do
- Explain rules briefly
- Document serious incidents
- Use warnings where appropriate
- Escalate uncertainty

## Do not
- Publicly argue
- Share private member information
- Invent answers
- Apply rules selectively
- Make commitments outside authority

## Shift handoff
Include unresolved issues, banned/restricted users, active incidents, pending support and important community sentiment.
