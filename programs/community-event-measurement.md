# Community Event Measurement Framework

## Event funnel
**Invited → Registered → Attended → Engaged → Converted → Returned**

## Metrics
- Invitation reach
- Registration rate
- Attendance rate
- Peak concurrent attendance
- Questions/comments
- Average watch/listen time
- CTA completion
- New member joins
- 7-day return rate
- Sentiment

## Qualitative signals
- Quality of questions
- Repeated themes
- Confusion
- Speaker relevance
- Community energy

## Reporting
End every event report with:
1. What happened
2. Why it mattered
3. What members told us
4. What we should do next
