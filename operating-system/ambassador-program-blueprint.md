# Ambassador Program Blueprint

## Purpose
Turn high-trust members into structured advocates, contributors and community multipliers.

## Selection criteria
- Consistent participation
- Strong communication
- Alignment with community values
- Helpful peer behavior
- Reliability
- Relevant reach or expertise

## Ambassador tracks
- Community support
- Content
- Events
- Local chapters
- Partnerships
- Education
- Social amplification

## Operating model
**Nominate → Vet → Onboard → Brief → Activate → Recognize → Review**

## Incentives
Prefer a balanced mix:
- Access
- Recognition
- Learning
- Leadership opportunities
- Performance-linked rewards where appropriate

## Guardrails
- Clear scope
- Brand guidelines
- Disclosure requirements
- Escalation channel
- Performance review cadence
