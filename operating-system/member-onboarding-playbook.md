# Member Onboarding Playbook

## Goal
Move a new member from **joined** to **oriented** to **first meaningful action** as quickly as possible.

## First 24 hours
- Welcome the member
- Explain the community's purpose
- Show the most important channels/resources
- Set expectations and rules
- Give one clear first action
- Provide a human support path

## Activation checklist
A member is considered activated when they complete at least one meaningful action such as:
- Introduce themselves
- Attend a live session
- Ask/answer a useful question
- Complete a product action
- Join a campaign
- Submit a report/contribution
- Refer another relevant member

## Messaging structure
**Welcome → Why this matters → What to do now → Where to get help**

## Metrics
- Join-to-activation rate
- Time to first meaningful action
- Onboarding completion
- First-week retention
- Top onboarding friction
