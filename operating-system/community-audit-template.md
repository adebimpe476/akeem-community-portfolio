# Community Audit Template

A practical diagnostic for evaluating the health, clarity and growth potential of a community.

## 1. Purpose
Clarify why the community exists, what members should gain, and what business outcome it supports.

## 2. Audit dimensions
| Dimension | Questions | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning | Is the value proposition clear? | 1–5 |
| Onboarding | Can a new member understand what to do first? | 1–5 |
| Activation | Is there an obvious first meaningful action? | 1–5 |
| Engagement | Are there recurring reasons to participate? | 1–5 |
| Retention | Are inactive members identified and re-engaged? | 1–5 |
| Moderation | Are rules, escalation and trust systems clear? | 1–5 |
| Feedback | Does member insight reach product/operations? | 1–5 |
| Analytics | Are growth, activation and retention measured? | 1–5 |

## 3. Evidence to collect
- Welcome messages and onboarding flow
- Weekly activity trend
- Top recurring questions
- Moderation incidents
- Campaign performance
- Event attendance
- Inactive-member cohorts
- Member sentiment
- Referral sources

## 4. Output
End the audit with:
1. Top 3 strengths
2. Top 3 risks
3. Highest-leverage opportunity
4. 30-day action plan
5. Owner and success metric for every action
