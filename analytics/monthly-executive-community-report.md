# Monthly Executive Community Report

## 1. Business impact
Summarize how community activity supported growth, product adoption, retention, partnerships or customer insight.

## 2. KPI movement
Compare:
- Community size
- Active members
- Activation
- Retention
- Campaign conversion
- Event participation
- Referral/advocacy activity
- Sentiment
- Support/moderation load

## 3. What changed
Explain the reasons behind movement, not just the numbers.

## 4. Member intelligence
Top recurring:
- Needs
- Complaints
- Product questions
- Content requests
- Partnership opportunities

## 5. Risks
List only material risks with impact and proposed mitigation.

## 6. Next-month priorities
No more than five, each with an owner and measurable outcome.
