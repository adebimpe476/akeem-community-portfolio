# Launch Community Dashboard

Use during product launches, campaigns or major ecosystem announcements.

## Pre-launch
- Waitlist / registrations
- Community readiness
- FAQ completeness
- Moderator readiness
- KOL/partner commitments
- Sentiment baseline

## Launch-day
- New joins
- Active members
- Questions
- Support volume
- Campaign participation
- KOL/partner mentions
- Conversion
- Incidents

## 7-day post-launch
- Retained new members
- Most common questions
- Drop-off points
- Content performance
- Sentiment change
- Product/community feedback

**Fix immediately:**
